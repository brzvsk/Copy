prepared for Copy. Each output is stereo 44.1 kHz PCM with an overall RMS level of
approximately -25.5 dBFS.

## Processing

Each source was trimmed to its short audible transient, converted to stereo
44.1 kHz 16-bit PCM, and gain-adjusted to approximately -25.5 dBFS RMS. The
bundled WAV files are derivatives intended only for use as Copy's UI feedback;
they are not distributed as standalone stock audio.
